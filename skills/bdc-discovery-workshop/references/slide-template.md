# Slide Template

Use this reference when the founder wants to turn workshop outputs into a presentation or concise deck outline.

## Workshop Deck Structure

Use this structure when no specific deck template is provided:

1. Produce your idea maze.
2. Idea maze template.
3. Map out the landscape.
4. Landscape template with value and usage axes.
5. Outline your JTBD.
6. JTBD template across horizontal tools, industry specific tools, and marketplace/network categories.
7. Opportunity surface area using the TPG framework.
8. Opportunity surface area template with problem type, tangibility, willingness to pay, growth, and KUA/AUV fields.
9. Create your "Meet ICP" slide.
10. ICP template with description, pain points, and status quo.
11. Champion message prompt.
12. Champion message template with three points.
13. List out 10 hypotheses.
14. 10-hypothesis template.
15. Find your first 10 die-hard customers.
16. GTM channel/experiment template.
17. Contact slide.

## Recommended Slide Content

For an idea maze output:

- Entrance to the maze
- Major decision branches
- Chosen path
- Why now
- Past casualties and lessons
- Dead-end risks and watch signals

For a hypothesis-testing output:

- User and scene
- Job statement
- Current alternative
- Pain evidence
- Riskiest assumption
- Falsifiable hypothesis
- Two-day test
- Decision rule and next step

## Writing Rules

- Keep slide text concise.
- Prefer concrete claims and evidence over narrative.
- Use one claim per slide where possible.
- Do not turn unresolved assumptions into confident statements.
- Keep open risks visible.
